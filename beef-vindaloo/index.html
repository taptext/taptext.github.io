Beef Vindaloo

Ingredients

  • 1 KG Beef
  • Spices: 2 cinnamon sticks, 1 TBSP fennel seeds, 2 tsp black pepper, 8 cardamom, 10 cloves, 1/4 tsp fenugreek seeds, 1 star anise, 5 dried red chillies, 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • Essence: 25 cloves of garlic, 2 pieces of ginger, a small bark of drumstick tree
  • 4 onions
  • Powders: 1/2 tsp turmeric powder, 2 TBSP kashmiri chilly powder
  • 4 TBSP Vinegar
  • Some curry leaves
  • Salt as required
  • A little sugar to balance the taste
  • 2 tbsp coconut oil

Instructions

  1. Dry roast every spice except mustard, put the mustard seeds only after turning the flame off, once roasted grind them in the mixer.
  2. Roast the onions in coconut oil and add the spice powder, wait for some time and then add the powders.
  3. Once the powders are roasted put everything back in the mixer and make a paste by adding some water.
  4. Mix the beef with the paste, sugar, salt, vinegar and curry leaves and leave it for an hour.
  5. Pressure cook by adding some water and the vindaloo is ready.
